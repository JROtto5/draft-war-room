# The in-season playbook

- **Season HQ** replaces the draft hero once pick 16 lands: title odds, next
  opponents, 📡 waiver radar with FAAB suggestions, 📰 your players' news,
  🏥 IR stashes, 🪓 drop candidates. Widgets are toggleable in Settings.
- **📅 Week recap** pulls live weekly scores for your roster (copyable).
- **🔔 Notifications** batch multiple status changes into one digest.
- **⇄ Bundle analyzer** (report modal) values 2-for-1 style trades.
- **📦 Archive**: finishing a draft stores your roster + projections snapshot
  in profiles — next summer, `tools/refresh.sh` brings the new season and the
  archive shows how the projections aged.
- Data refresh cadence: monthly is plenty; injuries/news/trends stream live.
