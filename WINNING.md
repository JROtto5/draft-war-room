# WINNING.md — the doctrine

The roster was built right (best total value in the league, receipts in the
Ego deck). From here, championships are process. This is the process.

## The weekly loop

| Day | Move | Tool | Key |

## Why this wins

- **Win probability over points.** Favored → floor lineup. Underdog → ceiling
  lineup, stacks welcome. The Plan flips automatically and says so.
- **The sim beats the eyeball.** 1,000 correlated Sundays > one gut feeling.
  The variant table settles every flex argument by win rate.
- **Points live on the wire.** Heat alerts + upgrade pairs + FAAB intel mean
  the wire is a weapon, not a chore. Bid suggester prices the panic of others.
- **The opponent beats themselves.** The sloppiness index proves it. The
  exploit finder schedules it.
- **Weeks 15–17 are the season.** Playoff-week eaters and the deadline stance
  keep every move pointed at the title game.
- **Process over results.** Management grade scores decisions, never luck.
  The autopsy is one paragraph; the bright side is data; then we reload.
