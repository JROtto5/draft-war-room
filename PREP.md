# Draft prep, the War Room way

1. **Settings** — slot, league names, 💖 favorite state/college, rival, risk appetite, pick timer.
2. **Study** — click through player cards (story, 3-year arc, environment). `C` compares, `Q` queues, boost/fade the ones you have opinions about.
3. **Queue** — order your targets; add "by round" tags so ⏰ warns when the window is closing.
4. **Plan** — 📌 pin one target per round on the card (or seed from queue). The plan board tracks got/sniped/passed live, and the on-clock banner reminds you when your pin is available.
5. **Rehearse** — 🎲 Mocks for full runs, 🧪 Scenarios to compare QB-QB vs RB-RB vs WR-WR openings from slot 12.
6. **Export prep** — Settings → prep file, so your phone has the same notes as the laptop.
Draft day: flip 🔴 LIVE and trust the board.
